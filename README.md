# Bank Customer Churn Power BI Analysis

An interactive Power BI data analytics project designed to evaluate bank performance, analyze customer churn patterns, explore demographic and behavioral trends, and monitor key financial metrics.

---

## 📊 Project Overview
The primary objective of this project is to analyze historical banking data to understand why customers leave the bank (Churn), identify high-risk customer segments, and support data-driven decision-making for customer retention[cite: 4].

---

## 🚀 Key Dashboards & Pages Included

### 1. Executive Overview
* High-level summary of overall bank and customer performance[cite: 4].
* **Key Metrics:** Total Customers (10K), Total Balance (764.86M), Churn Rate (20.37%), and Average Credit Score (650.53)[cite: 1].
* **Visuals:** Customer regional distribution (France, Germany, Spain) and churn age distribution[cite: 1].

### 2. Customer Demographics & Behavior
* Analysis of customer demographics, tenure, and engagement contributions[cite: 4].
* **Key Metrics:** Gender distribution (5K Male, 5K Female), Credit Card Holders (7K), and Active Members (5K)[cite: 2].
* **Visuals:** Gender-wise churn analysis, credit score impact, and account tenure trends[cite: 2].

### 3. Financial & Activity Analysis
* Evaluation of customer financial exposure and activity status[cite: 5].
* **Key Metrics:** Average Balance (76.49K), Active Members % (51.51%), and Total Churned Balance (185.59M)[cite: 3].
* **Visuals:** Balance distribution by number of products and active member status against churned accounts[cite: 3].

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
* **Data Visualization:** Power BI Desktop
* **Data Processing & Modeling:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions), Power Query
* **Dataset:** Bank Customer Churn Records (CSV format)[cite: 4]
* **Documentation:** Microsoft Word / PDF Report[cite: 4]

---

## 📈 Key Insights & Business Recommendations
* **Inactive Members:** Inactive members show a significantly higher tendency to exit the bank compared to active members[cite: 5, 6].
* **Age Brackets:** Customers within the **40 to 50** age group exhibit higher churn rates[cite: 1, 5].
* **Recommendations:** 
  * Implement targeted retention and engagement campaigns for inactive members[cite: 5, 6].
  * Focus special customer service initiatives on high-balance customer segments to prevent high-value churn[cite: 6].
